## What's being built

Beeline is the second portal I'm building from the operations-infrastructure recipe — same architectural backbone as Broomstick Hub, retuned for the specific shape of clinic operations.

The core insight is the same: most healthcare practices don't need another point solution. They need fewer tools, talking to each other, with the audit trail and the AI assist living *inside* the platform rather than bolted on.

## Why healthcare specifically

Healthcare ops has three problems that are well-suited to the operations-infrastructure approach:

1. **Multi-role workflows.** Practitioner, patient, ops admin, and finance all touch the same record from different angles. Standard SaaS forces each role into a separate tool.
2. **Compliance is real.** HIPAA-style audit retention isn't optional. The two-tier audit log pattern from bs-hub (contract retention 7y, operational 1y) maps directly onto healthcare requirements with minor tweaks.
3. **AI assist is high-leverage.** Triage questions, history summarization, scheduling anomaly detection — all places where a well-scoped AI in the loop saves hours per week without ever publishing patient-facing content unsupervised.
