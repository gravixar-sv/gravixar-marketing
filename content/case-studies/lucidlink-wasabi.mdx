## Cost and storage

Costs split two ways, live work and archive:

- **Live (LucidLink):** about 1.2 TB across 3 seats, 400 GB each, growing as the team grows.
- **Archive (Wasabi):** finished projects, final renders, and anything kept long term. The cost per gigabyte is much lower than Dropbox.
- **Policy:** don't keep large RAW footage in live storage unless it is needed. Render, then archive. RAW gets archived or deleted.

The rule that paid off most: keep live projects lean and predictable, and archive renders and RAW separately. Don't let the live set become a junk drawer.

## How it rolled out

Latency was the first thing to solve. After testing with LucidLink support, the team settled on the Frankfurt region, the best fit for where people sit. Ops led the testing, with hands-on help from LucidLink on server choice and storage planning as well.

Adoption took workshops and repetition, as it always does.

## House rules for every project

- Pin active projects, including the B-roll they need
- Keep the live set disciplined: don't pin everything "just in case"
- The same After Effects version on every machine
- Fonts and plugins installed on each machine, because the file system streams files, not installed software

## What's next

- Automate the move from LucidLink to Wasabi instead of doing it by hand
- Trigger it from monday.com, so marking a project "shipped" archives it
