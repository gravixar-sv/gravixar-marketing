The best example is running on this site. Every week, an AI checks what people are searching for, picks an angle, drafts a blog post, and drops it in a folder for review. Nothing goes live until I approve it.

That is the pattern I sell: AI does the boring 80%, and a person keeps the steering wheel for the 20% that matters. No "agentic" magic that publishes garbage at 3am. And you are not tied to one AI company. The model is set in one place in the code, so you can switch when a better one comes out.

## What it usually looks like

- **A smarter enquiry form.** A new lead answers 4 to 7 questions that change based on their answers, and AI writes a brand brief from their website. Your team walks into the first call already knowing the basics.
- **A nightly watch.** Every night it checks your activity log for work that is stuck, sudden floods of signups, bursts of deleted records, and anything else unusual. You get an email when something looks off, not when everything is fine.
- **Posts that wait for you.** Drafts line up for review. You approve once, and it posts to LinkedIn, X, and your blog. A person always makes the call.
- **A sorted inbox.** New messages get sorted by urgency, topic, and intent, with a suggested owner. A person confirms.

If you already run systems and want AI working inside them, this is the job.
