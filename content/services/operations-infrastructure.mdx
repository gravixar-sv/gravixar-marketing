## What I deliver

- A portal built around how your clients actually move through your business, not a template bent to fit
- A fixed path for every piece of work: draft, internal review, client review, approved
- Automatic assignment of who picks up what, based on workload and skills
- A record of every change to every item, with a safe undo for the fields you choose
- The boring parts that hold it all up: email templates, notifications, exports, and real control over who sees what
- Hiring and onboarding when the team grows: same portal, new tab

I build in small steps. In week one, you have something running. By week ten, your team has been using it for real work.

## Not only for agencies

The same foundation now runs a healthcare credentialing platform, live with 2,042 payer panels across 250 clients, and a booking system for a driving school, with the school's own rules (age limits, guardian consent, instructor availability) built into the core instead of added on later. If your business runs on records, approvals, and handoffs, the same shape fits. Only the industry changes.

For teams that already have a WordPress site and want it modernized rather than rebuilt, I will work in that codebase. The shape of the system matters more than the framework.
