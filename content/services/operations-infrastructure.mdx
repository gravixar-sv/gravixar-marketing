I build custom client portals for agencies and for accounting and law practices in the UK and the UAE, working remotely from my office in Pakistan. A build is $28,000 to $55,000, with the price fixed before the build starts, and a working portal typically takes 4 to 10 weeks. The one I'd point you to first is Broomstick Hub, the agency portal I built for Broomstick Creative, an agency working across the UAE and Pakistan. A new client there goes from first inquiry to an active project in about 10 minutes, where the same thing by email took two to three days.

You can click through a working portal right now at [demo.gravixar.com/lattice](https://demo.gravixar.com/lattice), with no sign-up. You play all three parts: submit a draft as the editor, pass it to the client as the project manager, then approve it as the client. Everything in it is sample data, and it's built separately from any client's system on purpose, so you can press every button.

## What I deliver

- A portal shaped around how your clients already move through your business
- A fixed path for every piece of work: draft, internal review, client review, approved
- A login for each client that shows only their own work, where they approve it
- Automatic assignment of who picks up what, based on workload and skills
- A record of every change to every item, with a safe undo for the fields you choose
- Email templates, notifications, exports, and real control over who sees what
- Hiring and onboarding when the team grows, in the same portal

I build in small steps. In week one you have something running, even if it's small, and by week ten your team has been using it for real work.

## Agencies

Two of the portals on my [work page](/work) run agencies. In [Broomstick Hub](/work/bs-hub), every inquiry moves through 12 fixed steps to a live project, so its status is never a note someone typed, and every change is logged. AI picks the next intake question and drafts a brand brief from the prospect's website, and it never approves anything. The [page for agencies](/for/agencies) has the rest, including the [monday.com rollout](/work/monday-rollout-agency) that took Broomstick Creative's team from 10 people to more than 50 on one delivery system.

## Accounting and law practices

A practice needs mostly the same machinery from a portal. Each client sees only their own work, and every change is on the record. I haven't published a build for an accounting or law firm yet. The closest is [Beeline](/work/beeline), a healthcare credentialing and billing company, where status is worked out from each application instead of typed into a workbook, and each person starts the day with their own follow-up list, most overdue first. Every amount of money is stored with the exchange rate it was recorded at, so a report run again later matches the original line for line. The [page for accounting and law practices](/for/accounting-and-law-firms) covers what changes for a firm.

If your business runs on records and approvals, the same shape fits, and only the industry changes. If you already have a WordPress site and want it modernized rather than rebuilt, I'll work in that codebase.
