A form gets you a name and a budget. This gets your project manager a brief. The prospect writes a sentence or two, the wizard reads their website, drafts their tone, audience, what they sell and what is missing, then asks only about what is still unclear.

It is not tied to one AI company, so switching to a better model is a one-line change. If the AI is down, the wizard still collects the answers as a normal form and marks the brief as written without AI. A prospect is never left waiting on it.
