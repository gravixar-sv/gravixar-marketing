I'm Qamar. I build operations infrastructure and AI-augmented tooling for teams that want the system running before they pay for it.

## How I work

**One operator, one head down.** No agency middlemen, no account managers, no four-week onboarding. You talk to the person doing the work.

**Production-first, demo-second.** Whatever I sell you, I'm running a version of it myself. The portal at [demo.gravixar.com](https://gravixar-demo.vercel.app) is the same shape as the systems I build for clients. Log in, click around, decide if the workflow makes sense before we ever talk pricing.

**Iterative, not waterfall.** Week one you have something running. Week ten you have something that's been used in anger. I don't disappear into a four-month build cycle and emerge with a "v1" you've never seen.

**AI in the loop, not the driver.** The SEO agent that drafts posts on this site doesn't auto-publish anything. The case-study auto-drafter doesn't post anything to LinkedIn without me approving the copy. That's the pattern I sell. AI does the boring 80%, the human keeps the steering wheel.
