# OUTREACH-RESEARCH.md

Vertical analysis + search playbook for cold outreach. Designed so you (or a research VA) can populate a real prospect list in a few hours rather than weeks.

**Honest framing:** I haven't web-scraped a real-time list of 200 specific companies (without live web access I'd risk inventing names). What this doc gives you is the *targeting framework* + *exact search queries* + *seed list of real well-known names per vertical*, enough to run the searches yourself and populate the real list in an afternoon.

---

## How to use this doc

For each vertical:

1. **Read the ICP profile**, your ideal prospect's shape (size, tools, signals).
2. **Run the search queries** on LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, Crunchbase, Google. Each query is calibrated to surface companies with the specific operational pain Gravixar solves.
3. **Add to your CRM**, even a Google Sheet with `company / contact / vertical / source / status / notes` columns.
4. **Use the outreach hook**, the angle that resonates per vertical.

Aim for **30-50 qualified companies per vertical** before reaching out to any. Volume + targeting > speed.

## Vertical 1, Digital agencies (current sweet spot)

This is where Broomstick Hub started. You already have a working portal that other agencies want.

### ICP profile

| Signal | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Size | 5-30 person creative / digital / branding agency |
| Geography | Anywhere (you're remote-friendly), but English-speaking markets convert easier |
| Current stack | Notion + Slack + Drive + email = sprawl. Bonus signal: they post job ads for "Project Manager" or "Operations Lead" |
| Founder background | Designer-turned-CEO or first-time agency founder (more open to systems thinking than career operators who've already chosen tools) |
| Active signals | Hiring designers/PMs (growing), client portal complaints in Reddit r/agency, long Slack threads about "how do we track this" |
| Disqualifier | Already running Productive.io / Function Point / Rocketlane, stuck-in-tool, won't switch |

### Search queries

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator (best for finding decision-makers):**
- Industry: `Marketing Services` OR `Design Services`
- Headcount: `11-50`
- Job title: `Founder` OR `CEO` OR `Operations Director`
- Posted on LinkedIn in past 90 days about: "delivery," "client portal," "scaling ops," "growing pains"

**Google searches:**
- `"creative agency" "client portal" site:reddit.com 2025..2026`
- `"how do we track projects" agency site:reddit.com`
- `"scaling our agency" blog post`
- Filter by recent results to find currently-frustrated founders.

**Apollo / Crunchbase:**
- Filter: industry "Marketing Services" or "Graphic Design," size 11-50, founded 2018-2023 (post-startup, pre-stuck-in-enterprise-tools).

### Outreach hook

> Subject: How [Their Studio] handles client review handoffs?
>
> Hi [Name], saw [specific recent thing, a project ship, a hire, a post]. I built a custom portal for one studio (Broomstick Hub) that runs their inquiry → activation → review flow on a state machine instead of a Notion tab. They saved ~3 hours per project on PM ↔ client handoffs. Would a 15-min walkthrough be useful, or am I way off?
>
>, Q

Specificity wins. Reference something they actually did this month.

### Seed list (real names, verify before reaching out)

These are real agencies I can recall from training data, confirm current state before contacting:

- **Buzzworthy Studio** (US, design)
- **Made by Many** (UK, product design)
- **The Workshop** (US, branding)
- **Shopify Plus partner agencies**, Crunchbase has the public list
- **Webflow Enterprise partner agencies**, Webflow.com/partners
- **Framer expert agencies**, framer.com/partners

Run the LinkedIn / Apollo searches above to populate the rest.

## Vertical 2, DTC e-commerce / Amazon brands

You already have **Brand & Visuals** as a service line + an Amazon A+ background. Lean into that for the entry, sell operations infrastructure on the back-end.

### ICP profile

| Signal | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Size | $1M-$20M ARR brand. Big enough to pay for design + ops, small enough that the founder still cares about quality |
| Channels | Amazon FBA + Shopify (most common DTC mix). Bonus if they're on a 3PL with custom workflows |
| Categories | Beauty / wellness / home goods / pet, high-aesthetic verticals where brand visuals drive conversion |
| Hiring signals | "Brand Manager," "Creative Director," "Operations Coordinator", they're feeling the pain |
| Disqualifier | Pure Amazon arbitrage / private label resellers, no brand, no operations infrastructure spend |

### Search queries

**Amazon-side:**
- Search [Amazon Best Sellers](https://www.amazon.com/Best-Sellers/zgbs) per category, click into top 50 listings, find brand websites with "About" pages mentioning a small team.
- A+ content quality is a tell, bad A+ = brand has budget gap = warm lead for your Amazon A+ service.

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator:**
- Industry: `Consumer Goods` + `Cosmetics` + `Health, Wellness & Fitness`
- Headcount: `11-50`
- Job title: `Founder` OR `Brand Director` OR `Head of Creative`
- Tools posted (LinkedIn signals): `Klaviyo`, `Shopify Plus`, `Gorgias`

**Google:**
- `"founded in 2020..2024" DTC brand "small team" -corporate`
- `Shopify Plus brand interview` (Shopify publishes these, gives you team size + struggles)

### Outreach hook

> Subject: A+ refresh for [Brand]?
>
> Hi [Name], your [specific product] listing's A+ content is doing the heavy lifting but the visual hierarchy is fighting itself. I redo Amazon A+ + supporting brand assets for DTC brands at [size]. 3-5 day turnarounds, fixed-fee. Want a quick teardown of [specific listing] gratis?
>
>, Q

Free teardown is the move. Most founders don't have the bandwidth to evaluate vendors blindly.

### Seed list (categories to mine, not specific names)

- Amazon Best Sellers in Beauty → top 100, filter for non-conglomerate brands
- Shopify Made With list (`shopify.com/case-studies`), handpicked DTC brands of the right size
- Subreddits: `r/FulfillmentByAmazon`, `r/shopify`, `r/eCommerceSEO`, founders post their own brands
- Klaviyo's customer page, they feature small brands prominently

## Vertical 3, Professional services (legal, accounting, consulting)

This is where **AI Tooling** + **Operations Infrastructure** combine into the strongest pitch. Professional services firms have intake → engagement → delivery flows that look exactly like the agency funnel, but with more compliance overhead and bigger contracts.

### ICP profile

| Signal | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Size | 10-50 person boutique firm. Solo practitioners are too small; Big-Law / Big-4 are immovable |
| Sub-vertical | Boutique accounting (~10-30 people), boutique law (commercial / IP), management consulting, strategy advisory |
| Tools | Stuck on QuickBooks + Microsoft + email + a single shared drive. Bonus: they pay for ChatGPT Team but don't have anything wired into their workflow |
| Active signal | Just lost or hired an Office Manager / Operations Director, they're feeling the gap |
| Founder profile | Owner-operator who built the firm in 2010s, now wants to scale without hiring 5 more admins |
| Disqualifier | Already on Clio / Karbon / monday.com, switched, locked in, won't move |

### Search queries

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator:**
- Industry: `Law Practice` OR `Accounting` OR `Management Consulting`
- Headcount: `11-50`
- Job title: `Managing Partner` OR `Founder` OR `Director of Operations`

**Specific lookalike searches:**
- `"managing partner" "boutique" accounting practice, site:linkedin.com`
- `boutique [city] consulting firm "operations director" hiring`
- Smaller cities convert better, DC / NY / SF firms are oversold; mid-size markets are warmer.

**Crunchbase:**
- "Professional Services" industry, founded 2010-2018, 11-50 employees.

### Outreach hook

> Subject: Intake → engagement at [Firm Name]
>
> Hi [Name], most boutique [law/accounting/consulting] firms I talk to have an intake → engagement-letter handoff that takes 3-4 days because nobody owns the canonical version. I wired an AI intake wizard for one firm that cut it to 2 hours and gave the partner a brand brief before the discovery call. Worth a 15 min walkthrough? Won't pitch you anything if it's not a fit.
>
>, Q

The "won't pitch if not a fit" line works disproportionately well in professional services because they're tired of vendor calls.

### Seed list (search terms, not names)

- Vault Top 100 boutique law firms, filter for under-50 attorneys
- AICPA member directory, boutique accounting firms
- Top management consulting boutiques per industry vertical (Vault, Consulting Magazine)

## Vertical 4, Healthcare clinics (aligned with Beeline)

Beeline is in development for this vertical. Even if you're not selling the full Beeline platform yet, you can sell **operations infrastructure** as a precursor.

### ICP profile

| Signal | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Size | 3-15 practitioner clinic. Multi-location (2-5 sites) bonus, operations pain compounds. |
| Sub-vertical | Dental, physical therapy, mental health (cash-pay especially), specialty medicine, integrative / functional medicine. **Skip primary care**, too tied to insurance + EHR vendor lock |
| Tools | Stuck on a single EHR (Dentrix, WebPT, SimplePractice) + email + Excel. Hate their EHR but can't leave |
| Geographic | US first (regulatory familiarity, payment willingness). Canada / UK secondary |
| Founder | Practitioner-owner who runs the clinic. Not a private-equity-backed roll-up |
| Active signal | Clinic is hiring "Practice Manager" or "Patient Coordinator", operations gap is real and current |
| Disqualifier | Hospital-system-owned practices, buying decisions go to the parent system |

### Search queries

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator:**
- Industry: `Health, Wellness & Fitness` + `Hospital & Health Care`
- Headcount: `2-50`
- Job title: `Owner` OR `Founder` OR `Clinic Director` OR `Practice Manager`

**Google:**
- `"private practice" [specialty] "second location" "operations"`
- `[specialty] clinic "growing pains" blog OR linkedin post`

**ZocDoc / Healthgrades:**
- Cash-pay specialty practices that show up in those listings without insurance filters, usually self-managed clinics open to operational improvement.

### Outreach hook

> Subject: Operations layer for [Clinic Name]?
>
> Hi Dr. [Name], building a healthcare ops platform (Beeline) for clinics that have outgrown the standard EHR + spreadsheet stitch. Currently in beta with [TBD], open to a few more clinics if you're at the "second location stretches us thin" stage. 15 min walk-through if it's the right time?
>
>, Q

Honest about beta state. Healthcare buyers are rightly skeptical of vendor pitches; honesty differentiates.

### Seed list (categories)

- Cash-pay PT clinics (search: `cash pay physical therapy [city]`)
- Functional medicine practices (Institute for Functional Medicine member directory)
- Multi-location dental specialty (orthodontics, pediatric)
- Mental health group practices (3-15 therapists)

## Tooling & workflow

To run this efficiently:

| Tool | Use | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** | Primary search engine for decision-makers | $99/mo |
| **Apollo.io** | Email finder + free outreach automation | Free tier covers ~600 emails/mo |
| **Hunter.io** | Email verification | Pay-per-search |
| **Clearbit** (optional) | Enrichment for known domains | Free tier |
| **Google Sheet** | The actual prospect list | Free |

You don't need all of these. LinkedIn Sales Nav + Apollo + a Sheet covers 90% of cold outreach hygiene.

## Volume calibration

A reasonable goal for one vertical:

- **Find 100 candidates** (search work)
- **Qualify down to 30** (skim the obvious mismatches)
- **Reach out to 30** (personalized, 1 email + 1 follow-up)
- **Expect 3-5 replies, 1-2 calls**

If those numbers don't materialize after 30 well-targeted reaches, the **hook is wrong**, not the volume. Iterate the message before adding more prospects.

## Sequencing

If you're running this solo while shipping, **don't try all 4 verticals at once**. Pick one, run it for 4-6 weeks, see what closes, then expand.

Recommended order based on the current state of Gravixar:

1. **Digital agencies** (week 1-6), you have the working bs-hub case study, this is the strongest direct sell
2. **Professional services** (week 7-12), AI intake hook is differentiating, fewer vendors competing here
3. **DTC e-commerce** (week 13-18), needs the case study from the first DTC brand you close before scaling
4. **Healthcare clinics** (after Beeline beta launches)

## When to revise this doc

After 30 cold reaches, write a one-page retro:
- What hooks worked
- What didn't
- Which prospect signals turned out to predict good fit
- Update this doc

The targeting framework only gets sharper from real responses. Don't let this doc go stale, every quarter, prune dead leads, add new signals you've learned, sharpen the queries.
